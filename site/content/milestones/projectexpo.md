# About the event
Project Expo is a highlight where members showcase their projects, receive valuable and constructive feedback, and see what others have been working on. It's a celebration of our collective achievements, new learnings and a source of inspiration for future endeavors.

All the projects being displayed are open source so anyone from the audiance can contribute to the projects
